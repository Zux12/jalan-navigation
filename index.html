V4.5 — Congestion Boundary DetectionSYNTHETIC · LOCAL ONLY

Model where a queue begins and ends along the selected route. The queue tail is the first congested segment in travel direction; the queue head is the last. Simulated traffic only — not real crowdsourced readings.

Calculate a route, then run a scenario.

Rules for this test: each route segment is approximately 200 m; congestion is counted only with at least 3 independent simulated drivers and 2 minutes of sustained slowdown. Unconfirmed segments are not used to establish queue boundaries. Recovery requires repeated evidence; missing data is not proof that a queue cleared. Boundaries are approximate and may shift with the selected route.
